Our mission

The mission of the National Days of Prayer Movement in Europe is to encourage the establishment of a National Day of Prayer at the country level in every European country, fostering unity among the peoples of Europe through common prayer for their nations, freedom, the common good, peace, and a prosperous future for the European continent.

The case for a National Day of Prayer

Benefits of establishing a National Day of Prayer at the country level

A National Day of Prayer is more than a date in the calendar. Across Europe it has proved to be a point of unity for society, a strengthening of the state, and a shared platform for churches and religious communities.

For Society

  • Strengthening national unity and social solidarity.
  • Fostering a culture of mutual respect, trust, and responsibility.
  • Promoting moral and spiritual values.
  • Inspiring hope and resilience during times of crisis and challenge.
  • Encouraging charity, compassion, and service to others.

For the State

  • Uniting citizens around a shared vision for the nation’s future.
  • Strengthening national identity and social stability.
  • Promoting a culture of responsible citizenship.
  • Supporting peace, social cohesion, and national security.
  • Demonstrating respect for spiritual heritage and freedom of religion.

For Churches & Religious Communities

  • Advancing interfaith and interdenominational dialogue and cooperation.
  • Uniting believers in prayer for their nation and its people.
  • Strengthening the role of churches in serving society and local communities.
  • Creating a common platform for spiritual unity.
  • Affirming the importance of faith, hope, and moral values in public life.

Throughout history

From the Middle Ages to the present day

Spiritual and national leaders across Europe have called their peoples to unite in prayer during critical moments of challenge and decision. These initiatives strengthened social cohesion, inspired hope and faith, and in certain instances played a significant role in events that shaped the destiny of nations.

1940

United Kingdom — a story of victory

Four days after taking office, Winston Churchill declared a national day of prayer. Five days later came news of the successful evacuation of British troops from Dunkirk.

A European precedent

Ukraine — the first country in Europe

On 12 February 2025 the Parliament of Ukraine passed a resolution establishing 24 February as the National Day of Prayer — making Ukraine the first European country to recognise a National Day of Prayer at the country level.

The first National Day of Prayer was observed on 24 February 2025, within days of the law being adopted. A year later, on 24 February 2026, the day was marked by an interfaith prayer service at St. Sophia Cathedral in Kyiv — the first official, intergovernmental prayer of its kind, attended by the President of Ukraine together with heads of state and government from across the continent.
