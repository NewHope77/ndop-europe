For churches and religious communities

We help form interdenominational steering committees, share materials for a first national gathering, and connect you with communities in other countries that have already begun.

For parliamentarians and public institutions

We provide the legal precedents — Public Law 100-307 in the United States and the 2025 resolution of the Verkhovna Rada of Ukraine — together with model legislative language adaptable to national procedure.

For media

Photographs, the movement’s brochure and interview requests: please write to info@ndop-europe.org with “Media” in the subject line.
