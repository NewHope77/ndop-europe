Prayer Marathon 28/30

Twenty-eight days of continuous prayer across thirty European nations. Each day of the relay is dedicated to a country — to its people, its leaders, its freedom and its future. Select a nation on the map to see its day, its prayer focus and the status of the National Day of Prayer initiative there.

    How it works

    A relay that never breaks

    28 days

    The marathon runs for four continuous weeks. Every day carries its own nations, so that no day passes without a country of Europe being lifted in prayer.

    30 nations

    From Iceland to Moldova, from Portugal to Finland — thirty European nations, each with a named prayer focus prepared with local participants.

    One purpose

    That every European country would establish a National Day of Prayer at the state level — and that Europe would find unity, peace and hope in common prayer.

    Take a nation

    Carry one country in prayer

    Churches, communities and individuals can take responsibility for a nation’s day in the marathon — or step forward as coordinator for a country still marked open.
